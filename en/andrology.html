Men's Health: Erectile Dysfunction & Premature Ejaculation

Men's sexual health concerns are very common and treatable. Your consultation is completely private, and your treatment plan is tailored once the cause is understood.

When do you need it?

  • Weak erections or difficulty maintaining an erection
  • Premature ejaculation
  • Delayed fertility or an abnormal semen analysis
  • Heaviness or pain in the testicles, or varicocele
  • Infections or any symptoms in the genital area

How does the treatment work?

  • Confidential consultation with a full medical history
  • Lab tests or investigations if needed
  • Medication, shockwave therapy, local injections, or varicocele surgery — depending on the case
  • Follow-up until you reach the desired result

When will I see results?

It depends on the cause — the doctor will explain your plan and the expected duration during the consultation. After varicocele surgery, the semen analysis is repeated after 3 months.

How to prepare for your visit

  • Bring any previous lab results or scans
  • Write down the medications you're taking
  • You can book using your first name only

Important tips

  • Erectile dysfunction can be an early sign of diabetes, high blood pressure or heart problems — getting checked matters
  • Don't take sexual enhancement medication without a medical consultation
  • Bring any previous test results with you

Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation confidential?

Yes — all your information and consultation details are kept strictly confidential.

Do I need tests before the consultation?

Not necessarily — the doctor will decide what's needed during the consultation.
